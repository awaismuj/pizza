# Pizza Restaurant POS & Operations Platform

## A clearer way to run service

GenZ Pizza Land POS is an integrated point-of-sale and restaurant operations
platform for pizza shops and quick-service restaurants. It brings counter
orders, kitchen progress, customer online orders, menu management, inventory,
payments, and business reporting into one system.

The goal is simple: help staff serve customers efficiently while giving
managers a clearer view of daily operations.

## Customer service and order management

- **Dine-in and takeaway ordering:** Build orders from the menu, manage table
  service, and handle takeaway orders from the POS.
- **Online ordering:** Give customers a separate storefront to browse the
  menu and submit orders. Online orders appear in the POS for staff to review
  and accept.
- **Order progress:** Follow orders through preparation and fulfillment
  statuses, from new order to completion or cancellation.
- **Kitchen workflow:** Give kitchen staff an order queue and preparation
  status controls, helping front-of-house staff and customers stay informed.
- **Receipts and bills:** Produce printed order bills with item details,
  totals, discounts, and applicable payment information.
- **Order controls:** Authorized staff can update orders and statuses, with
  cancellation and stock-restoration rules handled by the system.

## Menu, pricing, and promotions

- Organize dishes into editable menu categories.
- Add dish descriptions, photos, prices, and selectable variants.
- Build and manage meal deals and customizable bundles.
- Offer customer coupon campaigns, including welcome, Friday, and birthday
  offers, subject to configuration.
- Enable fixed-amount special discounts for authorized staff. The discount
  amount and applying staff member are recorded with the order and shown on
  its printed bill.
- Keep pricing and discount calculations on the backend, rather than relying
  on totals supplied by a customer device.

## Inventory and recipe-based stock control

- Track ingredients and other stock items with units, quantities, costs, and
  reorder levels.
- Connect a dish to a stock item or define a multi-ingredient recipe.
- Deduct configured ingredient quantities when eligible orders are placed or
  accepted.
- Restore deducted stock when an eligible order is cancelled.
- View low-stock items and adjust inventory with staff access controls.

Accurate stock deduction depends on the restaurant entering and maintaining
correct ingredients, units, and recipe quantities.

## Payments and fulfillment

- Record order payment details and support the payment methods enabled for
  the deployment.
- Configure pickup and delivery options, delivery areas, fees, preparation
  times, and customer-facing fulfillment details.
- Connect online checkout to the same order workflow used by restaurant staff.
- Payment gateway and QR payment options depend on credentials, provider
  setup, and deployment configuration.

## Management, reporting, and staff operations

- View sales and order activity through dashboard summaries.
- Review daily and monthly profit information, expenses, and inventory
  reports; export supported reports for further review.
- Manage staff accounts with roles and configurable permissions for areas
  such as orders, menu, inventory, and reports.
- Maintain an audit history for selected account and permission changes.
- Use employee tools for staff profiles, attendance, leave, payroll workflows,
  and employee self-service, when configured.
- Track restaurant assets and related maintenance and depreciation records,
  when the asset module is enabled.

## Customer storefront

The customer-facing website can present the restaurant's menu, categories,
deals, and promotional slides. Customers can place orders without needing a
storefront account, and the site provides order-status updates. Optional
customer accounts and coupon campaigns are also available, depending on
configuration.

## Designed for controlled access

Staff see and use features according to their assigned role and permissions.
Administrative settings, employee management, and sensitive operations are
restricted to authorized accounts. The backend validates order prices and
discounts before saving orders.

## Deployment and tailoring

The platform includes a restaurant POS, a shared API and database, and a
customer storefront. Menus, prices, recipes, staff permissions, fulfillment
rules, payment providers, and optional modules need to be reviewed and
configured for each deployment. Integrations and operational processes should
be confirmed during a product demonstration and rollout discussion.

## In brief

**One connected system for taking orders, coordinating preparation, managing
menu and stock, serving online customers, and reviewing restaurant
operations.**
